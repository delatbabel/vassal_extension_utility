Pushed a small change: this branch no longer touches `ObfuscatingOutputStream`. Its only edit there was XORing a block at a time instead of a byte at a time, and #15121 is removing the XOR altogether (the `savedGame` entry becomes a plain XZ stream), so the tweak is moot and dropping it also removes the one conflict between the two branches. The writers here still construct an `ObfuscatingOutputStream`, which #15121 keeps as a deprecated wrapper that compresses the same way; once both are in, `writeGameFile` and `saveGameRefresh` switch to `GameState.compressSavedGame`.
